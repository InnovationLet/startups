InnovationLet Startups is the startups desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the rounds, the founders behind them, and the unglamorous operational work that decides whether a company lasts past the next funding cycle. No founder-hero worship, no vanity metrics. Just what was raised, from whom, and what it's meant to buy.

## What we cover

- **Funding**: rounds, terms, valuations, and the liquidation preferences nobody puts in the announcement.
- **Founders**: the people building companies, including the ones who didn't raise at all.
- **Growth**: go-to-market, pricing, retention, and the unit economics that make or break a plan.
- **Operations**: hiring, culture, and the internal systems that scale or buckle.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: filings, term sheets where available, and founder interviews we can attribute. When we cite a number, we link it. When a company won't disclose a valuation, we say so. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does InnovationLet Startups cover?**
Funding, founders, growth, and operations. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.

**How often is it updated?**
The desk publishes daily during the week. Subscribe to the newsletter for a weekly round-up.

**Can I pitch a guest post?**
Yes. Send a short pitch to hello@f9xr.org. We review submissions weekly and reply to every one.

**Where can I find the other categories?**
